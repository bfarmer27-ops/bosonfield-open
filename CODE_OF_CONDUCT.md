# Community rules

BosonField is for people learning and building AI video tools.

## Required conduct

- Help the person solve the stated problem.
- State what you tested and what you did not test.
- Critique work, not people.
- Credit original code, media, research, and ideas.
- Respect licenses, privacy, consent, and platform rules.
- Mark synthetic media when people could mistake it for a real person or event.
- Keep adult, hateful, harassing, deceptive, exploitative, and illegal content out of the project.

## Not allowed

- Threats, harassment, slurs, sexual attention, stalking, or publishing private data.
- Impersonation, fake proof, false benchmarks, or hidden paid promotion.
- Requests to bypass safety checks, account controls, copyright, or privacy.
- Unlicensed model files, media, code, or scraped private data.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may hide content, lock a thread, or remove a person from project spaces when the record shows a rule breach. Evidence is kept private when sharing it would expose personal data. A person may ask for one review by a maintainer who did not make the first decision.
