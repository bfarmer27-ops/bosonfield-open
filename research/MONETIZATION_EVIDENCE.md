# Monetization evidence — 2026-09-09

Research check: **2026-09-09T16:42:20Z**. Platform rules change. The signed-in creator dashboard is the final account-specific check.

## Plain answer

YouTube is the main view-income path for Spain. GitHub Sponsors, Patreon, and transparent project support can start before view thresholds, but identity, tax, bank, and legal setup are human checkpoints. TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and X are discovery paths until their Spanish account dashboards show a live money tool.

No payout-per-view estimate is used. Pay changes with audience location, ads, qualified views, fees, tax, and account standing.

## Current platform facts

### YouTube

**Entry rule:** Spain is a YouTube Partner Program country. Early YPP access requires 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in the last 90 days, and either 3,000 qualified long-form watch hours in the last 365 days or 3 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days; this unlocks memberships, Super Chat/Super Stickers, Super Thanks and Shopping where separately available. Full ad revenue requires 1,000 subscribers and either 4,000 qualified long-form watch hours in the last 365 days or 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days. Paid advertising campaign views do not count toward the thresholds.

**Spain note:** Spain is eligible for YPP, but AdSense identity, tax information, payment-method availability, payment thresholds and any withholding are handled through Google/AdSense. Ad availability and revenue depend on viewer geography and advertiser demand; no per-view estimate is used here. EU/Spanish VAT and income-tax treatment should be confirmed with a Spanish tax professional.

**Ways money can enter:
- Watch Page ads on long-form videos and live streams after full YPP approval.
- Shorts Feed ads after full YPP approval.
- YouTube Premium revenue share.
- Channel memberships, Super Thanks, Super Chat and Super Stickers.
- Shopping, sponsorships, affiliate links, paid workshops and BosonField products outside YPP.

**AI and originality rule:** YouTube requires disclosure through the AI-use setting when content meaningfully alters or generates realistic material, including making a real person appear to say or do something they did not, altering real events or places, or generating a realistic scene that did not occur. Non-realistic animation, minor edits and ordinary production assistance generally do not require disclosure. The 2025 policy clarification renamed repetitious content as inauthentic content and explicitly covers repetitive or mass-produced videos. Reused content remains monetization-sensitive unless the creator adds significant original commentary, education, transformation or other value. AI tutorials should demonstrate the creator's own process, narration, edits and results rather than mass-producing near-identical uploads.

**Official pages:
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/94522?hl=en
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278?hl=en

**Not yet proved:** Feature-level eligibility, Shopping availability, payment settings and enforcement decisions can change by account and country. YouTube's official pages do not promise acceptance merely because numerical thresholds are met.

### TikTok

**Entry rule:** The Creator Rewards Program terms require the creator to be at least 18 or the age of majority, reside in a country where the program is available, maintain an account in good standing, have at least 10,000 authentic followers, and have at least 100,000 authentic video views in the prior 30 days. Eligible Rewards videos must be original and publicly posted videos of at least one minute. The official terms opened live were the US terms; they do not establish that a Spain-based account qualifies.

**Spain note:** Do not assume Creator Rewards is available in Spain based on the US terms. TikTok's official requirement is residence in an enabled country, and the product/app eligibility screen is the authoritative check for a Spanish account. Payment-account ownership, tax documentation and local product availability are required. Spain-based BosonField should plan around brand deals, LIVE/community support and outbound traffic until the Rewards tab is visibly enabled.

**Ways money can enter:
- Creator Rewards Program, only if the Spanish account is actually offered the program in-app.
- LIVE gifts, subscriptions and other LIVE monetization where enabled.
- Series or paid content where available.
- TikTok One and direct brand partnerships.
- Affiliate or commerce features where available in Spain.
- Use TikTok primarily as discovery traffic to YouTube, Patreon, GitHub Sponsors or Open Collective until Spanish Rewards availability is confirmed.

**AI and originality rule:** TikTok requires creators to label AI-generated content containing realistic images, audio or video, including realistic synthetic or meaningfully altered depictions of people, places or events. TikTok may also apply automatic labels. AI labeling does not remove copyright, privacy, impersonation, safety or deceptive-content obligations. Creator Rewards emphasizes original content; copied uploads, watermarked reposts, artificial views, and formats such as Duets or Stitches are not a safe basis for Rewards eligibility.

### Twitch

**Entry rule:** The current Affiliate qualification target is 25 followers, at least 4 hours streamed, streaming on at least 4 different days, and at least 3 average concurrent viewers on 4 different days within the qualification window. Twitch Partner has no guaranteed numerical threshold and is an application/review program. Twitch monetization is primarily livestream/VOD-based rather than short-form view-based.

**Spain note:** Affiliate and Partner access, payout methods, tax interviews, payout thresholds and sponsorship tools can vary by country and payment method. Spain-based creators must complete Twitch payout and tax onboarding; Twitch does not guarantee a particular payout amount per viewer.

**Ways money can enter:
- Subscriptions and gifted subscriptions after Affiliate or Partner approval.
- Bits/Cheers.
- Ads during eligible streams.
- Twitch sponsorship tools and direct brand deals.
- Educational live workshops, build-alongs, office hours and community events that direct supporters to Patreon, GitHub Sponsors or Open Collective.

**AI and originality rule:** Twitch's general rules do not provide a separate universal AI-video monetization label requirement comparable to YouTube or TikTok. AI-generated material still must comply with Twitch Community Guidelines, Terms, copyright rules, impersonation/deception rules, advertising disclosure and any content-classification requirements. Do not stream copyrighted model assets, music or third-party tutorials without rights. Clearly identify synthetic presenters, voices or demonstrations when failing to disclose could mislead viewers.

### GitHub Sponsors

**Entry rule:** There is no follower, view or watch-time threshold. Individuals who contribute to open-source projects and live in a supported region can apply; Spain is listed in GitHub's supported regions. BosonField should qualify through genuine public repositories such as ComfyUI workflows, training scripts, nodes, documentation or reproducible educational assets. A sponsored profile must complete payout and identity/tax onboarding.

**Spain note:** GitHub lists Spain as a supported region. Personal-account sponsorships have no GitHub fee according to the official documentation; organization-account sponsorships can incur a fee of up to 6%, split between payment processing and GitHub service processing. Payouts require bank/payment information and tax compliance, and fiscal-host options may differ.

**Ways money can enter:
- Monthly sponsorship tiers.
- One-time sponsorships.
- Sponsor benefits such as issue triage, documentation, community calls or early access, provided they do not promise inappropriate support or violate project licenses.
- GitHub Sponsors buttons and links from BosonField videos, README files and documentation.

**AI and originality rule:** GitHub Sponsors is an open-source funding mechanism, not a synthetic-media publishing or ad-revenue program. No separate GitHub Sponsors AI-label threshold was found. AI-generated code, workflows or documentation should be accurately described, reviewed, license-compliant and not presented as human-authored if that would mislead sponsors. Spam, fraud, impersonation, copyright infringement and deceptive sponsor benefits remain risks.

### Patreon

**Entry rule:** There is no follower, view or watch-time threshold. A creator can start a page for free and offer monthly or annual membership tiers and one-time purchases, subject to Patreon's Terms, Community Guidelines, payment onboarding and content policies.

**Spain note:** Patreon supports international creators, but payment methods, currency conversion, payout fees, VAT/sales-tax collection and creator tax obligations vary by location. Patreon's current public pricing page says it is free to start and charges 10% of income plus payment processing, currency-conversion, payout fees and applicable taxes. Spain/EU VAT and income-tax obligations remain the creator's responsibility where applicable.

**Ways money can enter:
- Monthly and annual memberships.
- One-time digital-product purchases.
- Paid posts, downloadable workflow packs, project files and private tutorials.
- Community chats, DMs, livestreams and member-only office hours.
- Tiered support for beginner, builder and sponsor/community levels.

**AI and originality rule:** Patreon does not impose a blanket ban on AI-assisted creative work, but creators must comply with its AI policy, Community Guidelines, intellectual-property rules, impersonation/deception rules and any stricter adult-content rules. Label AI-generated or synthetic material when needed for transparency, obtain rights for training/output assets and avoid presenting synthetic people or voices as real. Repetitive, copied or rights-infringing uploads are unsafe even without a universal reused-content threshold.

### Open Collective

**Entry rule:** Open Collective has no follower or view threshold. A project or collective must create an account, choose an appropriate fiscal host or supported operating structure, complete verification/KYC and comply with host and platform requirements. It is a funding and transparent-expense system rather than a creator ad-revenue program.

**Spain note:** The fiscal host determines much of the legal, tax, payout and compliance experience. Current Open Collective documentation says host fees are typically 4% to 10% or sometimes higher, charged before payment-processor fees; Stripe, PayPal, Wise and bank-transfer fees vary by method, country and currency. Open Collective's documentation also describes a March 2026 subscription-pricing model, with legacy arrangements still possible for older accounts. Spain/EU VAT, donations, grants and income treatment require professional tax/accounting advice.

**Ways money can enter:
- One-time community contributions.
- Recurring contributions.
- Project sponsorships and grants.
- Transparent budgets and expense reimbursement through a fiscal host.
- Links from BosonField's open-source repositories, documentation, livestream descriptions and community.

**AI and originality rule:** No dedicated Open Collective AI-generated-content monetization rule was found in the official documentation. AI-related projects should follow the platform's Community Guidelines, accurately describe the project, disclose material use of synthetic assets where relevant, respect licenses and maintain transparent spending records. There is no universal reused-content or repetitive-video rule because Open Collective does not monetize views.

**Official pages:
- https://opencollective.com/
- https://documentation.opencollective.com/why-open-collective/how-open-collective-works
